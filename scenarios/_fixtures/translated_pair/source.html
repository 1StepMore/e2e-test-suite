Meridian Robotics — Product Overview

Meridian Robotics is a global manufacturer of industrial automation systems. Founded in 2008, the company specializes in collaborative robotic arms, vision-guided assembly cells, and AI-driven quality inspection platforms.

1. Company Background

Meridian Robotics employs over 2,400 engineers and technicians across research centers in Boston, Munich, Shenzhen, and Bangalore.

2. Product Lineup

  • MR-Series collaborative arms
  • VX-Series vision systems
  • AI-Quality inspection platform

3. Target Industries

Automotive, electronics, pharmaceutical, food and beverage, aerospace.
